# Minors

- No trackers, analytics or third-party scripts in anything a child sees.
- Collect the minimum: a child account has a name and a login, nothing else by default.
- Spending rules flag, they never block (GLOSSARY: Flag).
- A parent's correction is visible to the child, with the parent's name.
- No 2FA for children (D-36). Changes to these flows need the legal review task (P7-35) to stay valid.
